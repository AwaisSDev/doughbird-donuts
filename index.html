Our dough

A 36-hour dough and a very loud fryer.

The dough starts the day before yesterday. Slow cold ferment, local butter, flour from a mill two hours up the gorge. No shortcuts, no mixes, and nothing fried ahead of time and held under a lamp.

We fry from six in the morning until the case is empty, which on a sunny Saturday means about eleven.

If it is not worth getting up at four for, it does not go in the case.

Marisol Vega — founder & head fryer
